Why it matters

The AI story today is less about any single model and more about how the industry is financed, governed, and used. L.A. Unified's shutdown shows that adoption is still being negotiated in real institutions, while Anthropic's IPO and Nvidia's leadership crisis force a harder look at the economics. Retirement and discoverability pieces are reminders that AI is now a personal-finance and professional-identity issue, not only a tech one.

Hot take

This is not a bubble in the old sense, but it is a financing bubble. The gap between the valuation and hype around AI and the still-thin path to profitability is the real risk. School districts and investors are both starting to price that gap, and the ones who ignore it will be the ones caught when it closes.

What to watch next

Watch how professionals and small businesses respond to being findable through AI rather than through traditional search.
