Why it matters

Today's stories cluster around three chokepoints: hardware supply, user data, and financing. Nvidia's Southeast Asia pause shows how export controls can reshape global supply chains overnight. Character.AI's bankruptcy sale, challenged by 42 state AGs, shows how personal data can be treated as a bankruptcy asset. And Anthropic's IPO, paired with Nvidia's governance questions, shows that the AI boom's economics are now as visible to markets as the technology itself.

Hot take

The AI industry's biggest risk right now is not a safety scare; it is a paperwork problem. Chips, chats, and credit lines are all getting regulated, audited, or downgraded at the same time. The companies that survive the next year will be the ones that treat governance, data consent, and financing discipline as product features, not legal chores.
